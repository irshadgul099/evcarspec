Tesla Model Y — Full Specifications, Range & Global Price

The Tesla Model Y is the world's best-selling electric crossover SUV, combining high energy efficiency, access to Tesla's Supercharger network, and generous cabin cargo space. Available in Rear-Wheel Drive (RWD), Long Range All-Wheel Drive (AWD), and Performance variants, it utilizes LFP chemistry in the base trim and high-density NMC battery packs in the dual-motor versions.
