✓ Buy This If...

You want supreme 6-seater luxury, rear-seat entertainment screens, air suspension, and battery-smooth EV driving without ever worrying about public charging stations on long interstate trips.

✕ Skip This If...

You live in a tight urban core with narrow parking garages or strictly prefer a pure battery-electric (BEV) architecture with zero gasoline engine maintenance.

The Li Auto L9 Ultra EREV bridges the gap between pure electric refinement and absolute long-distance touring freedom. Operating with a 52.3 kWh CATL ternary lithium battery, it provides 215 km of pure electric range that easily handles daily school runs and office commutes on silent battery power alone. When weekend family road trips beckon, the 1.5-liter turbocharged range extender seamlessly generates electricity on the fly, eliminating public charging queues entirely.

On freezing winter highway runs at 120 km/h with cabin heat and massage seats operating simultaneously, expect an energy consumption of around 22.5 kWh/100km alongside efficient fuel generation, yielding a rock-solid real-world highway cruising distance exceeding 1,100 km. Cabin luxury rivals vehicles twice its price, featuring zero-gravity ottoman seats, dual roof-mounted entertainment displays, and dual-chamber air suspension that smooths out rough terrain effortlessly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the pure electric range of the Li Auto L9 Ultra EREV? ▼

The Li Auto L9 Ultra provides 215 km of pure electric range (CLTC) on its 52.3 kWh CATL ternary lithium battery pack, covering typical daily urban commutes without engaging the 1.5-liter internal combustion range extender.

How does the Extended-Range Electric Vehicle (EREV) system work? ▼

Unlike traditional plug-in hybrids that use a mechanical transmission connecting the engine to the wheels, the L9 EREV's 1.5T turbocharged engine acts exclusively as a high-efficiency electrical generator to recharge the battery pack or power the dual electric motors directly. The wheels are 100% electrically driven at all times.

What is the total combined range with a full battery and full fuel tank? ▼

Under CLTC testing standards, the combined range reaches an astonishing 1,412 km. In real-world highway driving with climate control active, drivers routinely achieve over 1,120 km before needing both a recharge and a fuel stop.

Does the Li Auto L9 support V2L (Vehicle-to-Load) external power discharge? ▼

Yes, it supports up to 3.5 kW of external AC discharge power via V2L adapters, allowing you to run household appliances, induction cooktops, camping gear, or act as an emergency home backup inverter during power outages.

What are the charging times for DC fast charging and AC home charging? ▼

DC fast charging from 10% to 80% takes approximately 30 minutes at a peak charging rate of 75 kW. AC home wallbox charging at 7.0 kW takes roughly 7 hours for a full 0-100% recharge.

How spacious is the interior and what is the seating capacity? ▼

The L9 Ultra is a luxurious full-size 6-seater SUV featuring captain's chairs in the second row with massagers, footrests, and dedicated entertainment screens, alongside a spacious trunk capacity of 640 liters with all rows up.

What is the power output and acceleration of the dual-motor setup? ▼

The dual permanent magnet synchronous motors deliver a combined 330 kW (449 horsepower) and 620 Nm of torque, propelling this large SUV from 0 to 100 km/h in just 5.3 seconds.

What suspension system does the L9 Ultra use? ▼

It comes standard with dual-chamber air suspension paired with Continuous Damping Control (CDC), providing an exceptionally plush, magic-carpet ride quality that automatically adjusts to road surfaces.

What safety and driver assistance features are included? ▼

The Ultra trim is equipped with advanced ADAS hardware including roof-mounted LiDAR, high-definition cameras, millimeter-wave radars, and dual NVIDIA Orin-X chips supporting high-speed and urban autonomous navigation features.

What is the battery warranty offered on the Li Auto L9 Ultra? ▼

Li Auto provides a comprehensive 8-year or 160,000 km warranty covering the high-voltage battery pack, electric motors, and core electronic control units.
