About EVCarSpec.com

Welcome to EVCarSpec.com, an independent, technical specifications database and comparison platform dedicated to electric mobility. Our mission is to help everyday car buyers, fleet operators, and automotive enthusiasts cut through confusing manufacturer marketing claims by providing standardized, transparent data across Pure Electric Vehicles (BEV), Plug-in Hybrids (PHEV), Self-Charging Hybrids (HEV), Electric Two-Wheelers, and Commercial EV Buses.

Why We Built EVCarSpec

When shopping for an electric vehicle across different global regions, consumers face inconsistent testing standards. A vehicle sold in Europe quotes WLTP range, the same car in North America quotes EPA figures, while models launched in China or India rely on CLTC or MIDC laboratory cycles. Furthermore, manufacturers often highlight gross battery capacity rather than usable energy, and peak charging speeds rather than average 10% to 80% charging times.

EVCarSpec.com solves this problem by normalizing technical specifications into a single, easy-to-compare format backed by multi-currency pricing across the United States (USD), United Kingdom (GBP), European Union (EUR), India (INR), United Arab Emirates (AED), and China (CNY).

Our Data & Real-World Range Methodology

Every specification sheet published on EVCarSpec is compiled and cross-verified using official OEM homologation documents, regulatory filings (EPA, WLTP, MIIT, ARAI), and empirical telemetry data. Here is how we standardize key metrics:

🛣️ Real-World Range Model

Alongside official WLTP/EPA ratings, we calculate a standardized Real-World Mixed Range and a 110 km/h (68 mph) Highway Range based on usable battery capacity (kWh), aerodynamic drag coefficient (Cd), frontal area, curb weight, and HVAC thermal efficiency.

🔋 Gross vs. Usable Battery

We explicitly separate total lithium-ion pack capacity from net usable buffer, and identify cell chemistry—such as Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP / Blade) versus Nickel Manganese Cobalt (NMC / NCA)—so owners understand daily charging limits.

⚡ Charging Curve Analysis

Instead of only listing peak kilowatt numbers, our reviews evaluate nominal system voltage (400V vs. 800V architectures) and real 10% to 80% State of Charge (SoC) durations on DC fast chargers and 11 kW home AC wallboxes.
