About Our Platform

About EVCarSpec.com

Welcome to EVCarSpec.com, an independent global technical database dedicated to Electric Vehicles (EVs)—including passenger cars, electric SUVs, pickup trucks, and electric two-wheelers. Our mission is to simplify complex EV data so buyers, enthusiasts, and researchers worldwide can compare electric vehicles transparently in one place.

Our Data & Specification Methodology

Electric vehicle specifications are often confusing because manufacturers use different testing protocols across regions. At EVCarSpec, we standardize every vehicle profile using clear, comparable metrics:

  • Gross vs. Usable Battery Capacity: We clearly distinguish between total battery pack size and net usable energy (kWh), along with cell chemistry (LFP, NMC, or Solid-State).
  • Standardized Range Figures: We report official WLTP (Europe/UK), EPA (United States), CLTC (China), and MIDC (India) ratings alongside realistic highway range estimates.
  • Charging Performance: We track both onboard AC charger limits (kW) and peak DC fast-charging power, including 10% to 80% charging times.
  • Multi-Region Pricing: Our database tracks base and trim-level retail prices across the United States, United Kingdom, European Union, China, the Middle East (UAE), and South Asia (India).

Editorial Independence

EVCarSpec.com is completely independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any automotive manufacturer. All technical specifications are compiled from official manufacturer press releases, regulatory filings (EPA, EEC, MIIT), and verified technical documentation.
