Understanding Electric Vehicle Specifications & Global Standards

🔋 Gross vs. Usable Battery (kWh)

Every electric vehicle battery has a total (gross) capacity and a usable buffer. Manufacturers lock 3% to 8% of the pack to prevent deep discharge and prolong battery health. EVCarSpec lists both figures alongside battery chemistry—such as durable LFP (Lithium Iron Phosphate) and high-energy-density NMC (Nickel Manganese Cobalt).

🛣️ WLTP vs. EPA vs. Real-World Range

Official driving range varies by testing cycle: the US EPA cycle is the most conservative and closest to highway driving, Europe's WLTP is approximately 10–15% more optimistic, and China's CLTC / India's MIDC cycles favor low-speed city driving. Always check our highway estimate before planning road trips.

⚡ 400V vs. 800V Fast Charging

Vehicles built on 800V architectures (like the Hyundai Ioniq 5, Kia EV6, and Porsche Taycan) can accept 240–350 kW DC fast charging, replenishing 10% to 80% in under 18 minutes. Standard 400V EVs typically take 25 to 35 minutes on high-power DC chargers.
