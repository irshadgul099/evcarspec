Understanding Electric Vehicle Specifications & Global Standards

🔋 Gross vs. Usable Battery (kWh)

Every electric vehicle battery has a total (gross) capacity and a usable buffer. Manufacturers lock 3% to 8% of the pack to prevent deep discharge and prolong battery health. EVCarSpec lists both figures alongside battery chemistry—such as durable LFP and high-energy-density NMC.

🛣️ WLTP vs. EPA vs. Real-World Range

Official driving range varies by testing cycle: the US EPA cycle is the most conservative, Europe's WLTP is approximately 10–15% more optimistic, and China's CLTC / India's MIDC cycles favor city speeds. Check our real-world highway range before planning road trips.

⚡ 400V vs. 800V Fast Charging

Vehicles built on 800V architectures can accept 240–350 kW DC fast charging, replenishing 10% to 80% in under 18 minutes. Standard 400V EVs typically take 25 to 35 minutes on high-power DC chargers.
