Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

At EVCarSpec.com (accessible from https://evcarspec.com/), protecting the privacy of our visitors is one of our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected and recorded by EVCarSpec.com and how we use and safeguard that information.

1. Information We Collect

You can browse our electric vehicle database, use our interactive comparison tools, and switch regional currencies without creating an account or submitting personal information. We only collect personal data (such as your name and email address) when you voluntarily contact us via email or our Contact Us page.

2. Log Files & Analytics

EVCarSpec.com follows a standard procedure of using server log files and privacy-preserving web analytics. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing traffic trends, administering the site, and improving user experience.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any modern website, EVCarSpec.com uses cookies to store visitor preferences (such as your selected currency or filter preferences) and to optimize the display of content based on visitors' browser types.

4. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie

  • Google is a third-party vendor on our website. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve advertisements to our site visitors based upon their visit to https://evcarspec.com/ and other sites on the internet.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie for personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads or by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights (European Economic Area & UK Visitors)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you are entitled to the following data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The right to access, update, or delete the personal information we hold on you.
  • The right of rectification if your information is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • The right to object to or restrict our processing of your personal data.
  • The right to withdraw consent at any time through our Cookie Consent banner or browser settings.

6. CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights (California Visitors)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), California consumers have the right to request that a business disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected, request deletion of personal data, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. EVCarSpec.com does not sell users' personal contact information.

7. Children's Information

EVCarSpec.com does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided this kind of information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us by email at contact@evcarspec.com or visit our Contact Us page.
